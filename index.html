Hi, I'm Sanika. Let me tell you a little bit about myself.

My background is a bit of a mix – publishing, books, social media, content, strategy, and client servicing.

I spent the early part of my career managing a children's bookstore, where I was involved in pretty much everything that kept the place going. I managed its social media presence, spoke to customers every day, curated the collection, handled inventory and orders, and worked with authors and illustrators to bring events into the store. I also helped curate book fairs at international schools and NGOs, as well as book selections for events at places like the Museum and Conscious Collective. It was a role that taught me a lot about books, people, and everything that happens behind the scenes to bring the two together.

From there, I moved into social media and content, working with healthcare and mental health brands, spaces where what you say, how you say it, and what you choose to leave unsaid can matter quite a bit. Alongside social media strategy and content, I've also worked on LinkedIn personal branding and ghostwriting for 12+ B2B SaaS founders across different industries, as well as long-form writing, brand and growth strategy, and account management.

Today, I sit somewhere between content, strategy, and execution. I like understanding what a brand is trying to do, figuring out how to communicate it, and getting the work from idea to execution.

A little varied, perhaps. But there's usually a connection between the dots.

Places I've Been, Things I've Noticed

I've travelled to 6+ countries so far, and one of my favourite things about travelling is getting a glimpse into lives so different from my own. The way people eat, spend their time, build their homes, or go about an ordinary day often tells me more about a place than its landmarks ever could.

Travel has a way of putting things into perspective. Things that feel overwhelming back home can seem a little less so when you're somewhere unfamiliar, seeing how differently the world works. I always come back with photographs, food memories, and a slightly different way of looking at things.
